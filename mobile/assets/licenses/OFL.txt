Provenance
----------
The .ttf files in `assets/fonts/` were taken from the Google Fonts
repository (https://github.com/google/fonts, `ofl/<family>/`). DM Sans,
JetBrains Mono and Noto Serif Devanagari ship there only as variable fonts;
the static weights bundled here were produced from those variable sources
with `fontTools.varLib.instancer` (pinning `wght`, and `opsz=14` / `wdth=100`
where those axes exist). No glyph outlines were modified.

Fonts are bundled as application assets and are never fetched over the
network at runtime.
