## 1. Kanda Poha
`breakfast` · Maharashtrian · tags: **veg, vegan, gluten_free** · serves 2 · prep 10 · cook 15
- 150 g — Poha (Thick)
- 1 piece — Onion (finely chopped)
- 1 piece — Potato (small cubes)
- 2 piece — Green Chilli
- 30 g — Peanuts
- 1 tsp — Mustard Seeds *
- 1 sprig — Curry Leaves *
- 0.5 tsp — Turmeric Powder *
- 2 tbsp — Vegetable Oil
- 1 tsp — Sugar *
- 1 piece — Lemon (juice)
- 2 tbsp — Coriander Leaves (chopped)
- 1 tsp — Salt *
1. Rinse the poha in a colander until soft; drain, sprinkle salt and sugar, and rest 5 minutes.
2. Heat oil, crackle the mustard seeds, then add peanuts and fry until golden.
3. Add curry leaves, green chilli and potato; cook covered until the potato is tender.
4. Add onion and turmeric; sauté until translucent.
5. Fold in the poha gently, steam 2 minutes, finish with lemon juice and coriander.

## 3. Dal Tadka
`sabzi_dal` · North Indian · tags: **veg, vegan, gluten_free** · serves 4 · prep 10 · cook 30
- 200 g — Toor Dal
- 1 piece — Tomato (chopped)
- 1 piece — Onion (chopped)
- 0.5 tsp — Turmeric Powder *
- 2 tbsp — Ghee
- 1 tsp — Cumin Seeds *
- 4 piece — Garlic (Clove) (sliced)
- 2 piece — Dried Red Chilli
- 0.5 tsp — Kashmiri Chilli Powder *
- 2 tbsp — Coriander Leaves
- 1 tsp — Salt *
1. Pressure-cook dal with turmeric, salt and 3 cups of water until soft; whisk smooth.
2. Heat ghee; add cumin, garlic and dried chilli; fry until the garlic is golden.
3. Add onion and tomato; cook until soft, stir in chilli powder, then pour over the dal.
4. Simmer 5 minutes and finish with coriander.
> Diet note: marked vegan only if ghee is swapped for oil — tagged **veg** only; vegan tag is added on the oil variant. *(Review item — see anomalies.)*

## 5. Chicken Rara (from the URL import you tried)
`sabzi_dal` · North Indian (Punjabi) · tags: **non_veg, gluten_free** · serves 4 · prep 20 · cook 45
- 300 g — Chicken (Curry Cut) (drumsticks/pieces)
- 250 g — Chicken Mince
- 3 piece — Onion (puréed)
- 3 piece — Tomato (puréed)
- 2 tbsp — Ginger Garlic Paste *(merged: 1 tbsp marinade + 1 tbsp gravy)*
- 100 g — Curd
- 1 tbsp — Kashmiri Chilli Powder
- 1 tbsp — Coriander Powder
- 1 tsp — Cumin Powder
- 1 tsp — Garam Masala
- 6 piece — Cloves
- 10 piece — Black Pepper (Whole)
- 1 piece — Cinnamon Stick
- 2 piece — Black Cardamom
- 4 piece — Green Cardamom
- 3 tbsp — Mustard Oil
- 1 tsp — Salt *
- 1 cup — Water *(0.5 cup marinade + 0.5 cup gravy, summed)*
1. Marinate the chicken pieces with curd, half the ginger-garlic paste, chilli powder and salt for 30 minutes.
2. Heat oil, temper the whole spices, add onion purée and cook until golden.
3. Add the remaining ginger-garlic paste, tomato purée and ground spices; cook until the oil separates.
4. Add the marinated chicken, then the mince; cook covered 20 minutes, add water as needed.
5. Finish with garam masala.
> What changed vs. the app's import: "gms chicken, 300 cup" → 300 g; `&quot;` removed; "salt or to taste" → Salt staple; the two ginger-garlic lines and two water lines are **summed**; spices listed as pieces ("cloves 6 cup") now use piece.

## 12. Gajar Halwa
`sweet` · North Indian · tags: **veg, gluten_free** · serves 4 · prep 15 · cook 40
- 500 g — Carrot (grated)
- 500 ml — Milk (Full Cream)
- 100 g — Sugar
- 3 tbsp — Ghee
- 2 piece — Green Cardamom
- 30 g — Cashew Nuts
- 30 g — Raisins
1. Cook carrot in milk, stirring, until the milk is absorbed (about 30 minutes).
2. Add ghee and sugar; cook until glossy and the ghee separates.
3. Finish with cardamom, cashews and raisins.
